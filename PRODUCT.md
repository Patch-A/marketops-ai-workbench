# MarketOps AI Workbench

## Register

product

## Platform

Independent, open-source web application with optional connectors.

## Users

Primary users are individual project owners in event, brand, and B2B marketing. The first release is optimized for one operator while preserving organization, client, and workspace boundaries for later team use.

## Product Purpose

MarketOps turns an approved marketing proposal into a traceable execution plan, monitors delivery and the assumptions behind it, and converts reviewed project outcomes into reusable knowledge for later projects.

## Positioning

An AI-native marketing project operations workbench that connects strategy, schedule, execution, market change, and learning without depending on a chat platform or a specific model provider.

## Product Boundaries

- The workbench is the product.
- Agents are constrained workers inside the product.
- Skills are repeatable procedures used by agents or contributors.
- Feishu, WeCom, email, calendars, and monitoring services are optional connectors.
- Project state, permissions, evidence, versions, and audit events remain authoritative.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and useful. The interface should feel like a capable project partner: direct about uncertainty, respectful of human approval, and never theatrical about AI.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic chatbot shells, feature-count dashboards, news-feed overload, fake ROI precision, invisible automation, and project templates that hide where their dates or assumptions came from.

## Design Principles

- Put the current project decision, dependency, or risk at the center.
- Make evidence, model hypotheses, human edits, approved decisions, and actual outcomes visibly different.
- Show how a market signal affects assumptions, deliverables, and tasks.
- Keep AI actions inspectable, reversible, and recoverable.
- Make project and knowledge scope visible before users save or reuse information.
- Never hide uncertainty, sources, cost, or the need for human approval.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, clear labels, and responsive use at 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
